The Campus
Treasure Hunt

An Odyssey of Faith & Discovery
✦

“Seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened unto you.”

Matthew 7:7

Each team is given one riddle at a time. Every riddle is a story from Scripture that points to a spot on campus. Work out where it means, walk there, and scan the QR code posted at that location to unlock your next riddle. There are twelve stops in all — clear them and race back to be the first team to finish. Every route begins and ends at the CSE Department.
